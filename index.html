Two New Apps

Two New Apps, and One of Them Is Built Just for You

BoatBites has two brand new apps, one for boaters and one for our delivery drivers. Both are live now on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

 

If you downloaded BoatBites before, the app on your phone is the old version. Please download BoatBites Boater in its place, and it will self-update to a brand new “look and feel.”

We used to run everything through one app. Boaters and drivers shared the same screens, and a lot of you told us it was confusing to switch between them. So we split them up. The boater app now has one job, and that’s getting food and drinks out to your boat.

Here’s what’s new for boaters:

✓ A cleaner app made only for ordering. No driver menus and no extra steps.
✓ Better boat finding. We rebuilt our marine routing and geofencing so drivers can find you at anchor, on the sandbar, or tied up at a marina.
✓ Drinks with your food. The new app supports beer, wine, and other drinks, so one order can cover everything. More on that further down.
✓ A stronger system behind the scenes. We rebuilt the backend that connects you, the restaurant, and your driver, so orders move faster and more reliably.
✓ Messages meant for you. Since boaters and drivers have separate apps, what you hear from us will be about ordering, not driving.

A lot of this came straight from our spring deliveries in Fort Myers. We watched where orders got stuck, listened to customers and drivers, and fixed those spots first.

Download BoatBites Boater now so you’re set when we’re back on the water this fall.

For Drivers

Know Someone With a Jet Ski and Some Free Weekends?

Our drivers have a new app too. It’s called BoatBites Delivery Driver, and it’s made only for the people who carry orders from shore out to your boat.

If you’ve thought about driving for us, or you know a captain who’d like to earn money on the water, now is a good time to look. Here’s what the new driver app offers:

✓ An app of their own. Drivers no longer share screens with boaters, so the tools they use every day are right up front.
✓ Navigation built for the water. Marine routing and geofencing help drivers find boats that are anchored, drifting, or tucked into a cove, instead of relying on a dock address.
✓ Insurance handled in the app. Drivers without their own coverage can sign up for daily insurance by tapping “I Acknowledge.” The daily fee comes out of that day’s earnings and only applies while they’re online.
✓ An option to bring your own policy. Drivers who already carry marine insurance can email it to Drivers@BoatBites.app and get it approved before their first run.
✓ Updates just for drivers. Messages from us go to drivers only, so they get schedules, reminders, and delivery news without the clutter.

Requirements vary by area, and we walk every new driver through them at signup.

Download BoatBites Delivery Driver, or forward this to a friend who spends more time on the water than on land.

Fort Myers & Captiva

Fort Myers and Captiva, We’re Coming Back This Fall

BoatBites returns to Fort Myers and Captiva this fall.

From February through May we delivered to boaters around Fort Myers on weekends, and it was our busiest season so far. Easter weekend brought in more orders than any other week of the year. Plenty of you ordered more than once, which told us we were onto something.

More Food Choices in the Fort Myers Area

When we’re back this fall, you’ll have more places to order from than ever. We’ve added many new food choices in the Fort Myers area, including Matanzas and Jersey Mike’s Subs. You can get fresh seafood and a cold drink from Matanzas, or a stack of subs that feeds the whole boat. More restaurants are joining every week, and we’ll share the full list before we launch. Download BoatBites Boater now so you’re ready to order on day one.

So what changes this fall?

✓ You’ll order through the new BoatBites Boater app.
✓ Your driver will have better tools to find you.
✓ The whole system behind your order has been rebuilt from the ground up.
✓ You’ll be able to add drinks to your order once our alcohol service goes live.

We’re lining up restaurants and marina partners now, and we’ll share the full list before launch. After Fort Myers and Captiva, Miami is next.
